Watermark or change stream?

      Chapter 3 · Redis

      A consumer group that survives a crash

      Each stream entry goes to one consumer of the group and stays in the pending entries list (PEL) until it is acknowledged. Redis never redelivers by itself: somebody has to claim a dead worker's entries. Play it, or replay the lab.

      Chapter 3, step 3.8 ↗

      Commands

        The rules here are Redis's: XREADGROUP ... > hands out entries no one in the group has received and records them as pending for that consumer; XACK removes them; XAUTOCLAIM moves entries idle longer than its minimum (1000 ms here) to the caller and adds one to their delivery count; a crashed consumer's entries just wait. The dead-letter rule (after 3 deliveries) is the lab's own, not a Redis feature.

        The rules the calculator uses
        
            
          
              
        • One read capacity unit (RCU) is one strongly consistent read of up to 4 KB, or two eventually consistent ones.
          • 
              
        • A query or scan adds up the size of every item it reads, then rounds up to the next 4 KB, per page.
          • 
              
        • A page holds at most 1 MB, then LastEvaluatedKey asks for the next one.
          • 
              
        • The filter runs after the read: ScannedCount is what you pay for, Count is what you get.
          • 
            
        
            
        DynamoDB Local reports capacity but never throttles, and its numbers were not compared with
              the service. Global secondary indexes
